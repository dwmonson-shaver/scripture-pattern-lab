# Assumptions Register

## ASM-001 — Serious users will value transparent evidence over black-box answers
- Confidence: High
- Risk if false: Product may overinvest in explainability at expense of convenience
- Source: CONV-001, CONV-002

## ASM-002 — Natural language entry will be the preferred default for most users
- Confidence: Medium
- Risk if false: Direct DSL authoring may deserve more investment earlier
- Source: CONV-004

## ASM-003 — Postgres plus pgvector is sufficient for an MVP
- Confidence: Medium
- Risk if false: Search and ranking quality may require earlier Elastic/OpenSearch adoption
- Source: CONV-002, EXT-002

## ASM-004 — DSL versioning will be necessary as feature surface expands
- Confidence: High
- Risk if false: Simpler ad hoc syntax may prove adequate for longer than expected
- Source: CONV-004, EXT-001

## ASM-005 — Cross-lingual alignment can be deferred until after MVP without undermining the core concept
- Confidence: Medium
- Risk if false: Early users may expect MT/LXX/NT comparison from the start
- Source: CONV-002
